# Aviana Travel — Policies & Traveler Guide

This is a fictional reference document for the Aviana Travel demo. It exists
to give the agent's `search_knowledge_base` tool something realistic to
retrieve — none of this is a real company or a real policy.

## Booking & Cancellation Policy

- Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours after purchase
  ("cooling-off window"), regardless of travel date.
- Outside the cooling-off window, cancellations made 14+ days before
  departure receive a full refund minus a $35 service fee per traveler.
- Cancellations made 3–13 days before departure receive a 50% refund.
- Cancellations made within 72 hours of departure are non-refundable, except
  for documented medical emergencies or bereavement, which are reviewed
  case-by-case.
- Date changes are free up to twice per booking if requested more than 7
  days before departure; a $25 change fee applies after that.

## Aviana Miles Loyalty Program

- Every dollar spent on flights or hotels booked through Aviana earns 2
  Aviana Miles; travel-insurance purchases earn 1 Mile per dollar.
- Tiers: Silver (0+ Miles), Gold (25,000+ Miles/year), Platinum (75,000+
  Miles/year).
- Gold members get free seat selection and priority check-in. Platinum
  members additionally get one free checked bag per flight and access to
  Aviana airport lounges.
- Miles expire 24 months after the date they were earned if the account has
  no qualifying activity in that window.
- Miles can be redeemed for flight and hotel discounts at a rate of 100
  Miles = $1, in increments of 500 Miles.

## Baggage Policy

- Economy fares include one personal item and one carry-on bag (max 8kg /
  17.5 lb) free of charge.
- A first checked bag (max 23kg / 50 lb) costs $30 on domestic routes and
  $45 on international routes if purchased at booking; $60 if added at
  check-in.
- Overweight bags (23–32kg) incur an additional $75 fee. Bags over 32kg are
  not accepted and must be split or shipped separately.
- Business and Platinum-tier travelers receive one free checked bag.

## Travel Documents & Entry Requirements

- Aviana does not verify visa or entry requirements on the traveler's
  behalf — travelers are responsible for confirming passport validity,
  visa, and vaccination requirements with the relevant embassy or consulate
  before departure.
- As a general rule of thumb (not a guarantee), most countries require a
  passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the return date; always
  confirm the exact rule for your specific nationality and destination.
- Aviana recommends purchasing travel insurance for any international
  itinerary, particularly for trips involving connecting flights with less
  than 90 minutes layover time.

## Group Bookings

- Groups of 8 or more travelers on the same itinerary qualify for group
  rates: a 10% discount on the base fare and one complimentary seat for
  every 20 paid seats.
- Group bookings require a deposit of $100 per traveler within 5 business
  days of booking, with the balance due 30 days before departure.
- Group bookings cannot be combined with the standard cooling-off
  cancellation window; group-specific cancellation terms are issued at time
  of booking.

## Customer Support

- Support is available via chat and email 24/7; phone support is available
  06:00–22:00 in the traveler's local time zone.
- Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days back to the original
  payment method.
- For disputes about a charge, travelers should contact Aviana support
  before initiating a chargeback — most issues are resolved faster directly.
